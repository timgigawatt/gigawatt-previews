Since 1952

Started by one family. Still run by the same one.

J.F. Denney opened its doors in 1952 when John Sr. and Dorothy Denney set out to serve Kansas City homeowners with honest, dependable work. Seventy-four years later, the company is in its third generation — owned and operated today by Mark & Becky Denney.

Longevity like that is rare in the trades, and it only happens one way: by showing up, standing behind the work, and treating your home the way we'd treat our own. That's why so many Johnson County families have called the same number for decades.
