Why Leawood families stay

Three generations. One promise.

Kiddi Kollege didn't start as a franchise — it started in a family's basement in 1957, built on the simple belief that every child deserves to feel loved while they learn.

The same family still runs it today. Geraldine's grandchildren grew up in these classrooms, taught in them, and now lead them. When you tour our Leawood center, you're not meeting a manager — you're meeting the family whose name is on the door.

That continuity is rare in childcare, and it's exactly why parents trust us with their most precious little people, year after year.
