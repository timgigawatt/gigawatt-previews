My dog ate a little chocolate — should I be worried?
It depends on the type and amount. Roughly how much, what kind (milk, dark, baker's), and how big is your dog? Dark and baker's chocolate are the most concerning. 🍫
A few milk chocolate chips, he's a 60 lb lab.
That's likely a low risk at that size — watch for vomiting or restlessness. Want me to book a same-day check with our team just to be safe?
