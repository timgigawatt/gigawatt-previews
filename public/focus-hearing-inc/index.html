Our story

A family name you can trust with your hearing

It started in 1971, when Lee F. Schneller opened his first hearing clinic in Garden City, Kansas. His son Michael grew up in that clinic — repairing and cleaning hearing aids after school and traveling with his dad to hospitals and retirement communities across the state.

Today Michael leads Focus Hearing, carrying a third generation of hearing-care expertise into Overland Park. That's more than 50 years of doing right by the people who walk through our door — because for us, they're neighbors, not numbers.
