Why Best Regards

A neighborhood legend, one cookie at a time.

What started as a search for a better cookie to put in a gift basket turned Robert and Cherrie Duensing into a Kansas City institution. Robert's baking earned him local fame as "The Cookie Man," a spot on Food Network, and a line out the door on Chiefs Sundays.

Thirty-plus years later, it's still a family affair — and everything is still made from scratch, with no mixes, no preservatives, and no shortcuts.
