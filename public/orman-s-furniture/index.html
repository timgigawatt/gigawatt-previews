Why Orman's

A furniture man since 1967 — and still choosing every board.

Tim Orman knows solid wood the way few people still do. Since 1967, Orman's has skipped the flat-pack particleboard and the big-box upsell in favor of real American hardwoods — brown maple, cherry, red oak, quartersawn white oak — built by skilled Amish craftsmen to last for generations.

Nothing here is take-it-or-leave-it. Pick the piece, the wood species, the stain, the leather or fabric, even the dimensions — and Orman's makes it to order at a price that beats the national chains.

✦ Brown maple & cherry ✦ Amish-built quality ✦ Honest, no-pressure advice ✦ Fair prices vs. big-box

— Tim Orman, owner
