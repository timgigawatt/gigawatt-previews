The 40-year story

Same corner. Same welcome. Four decades in.

For close to 40 years, Old 56 Family Restaurant has held down the same corner on S. Chestnut Street in Olathe — a friendly, down-home spot with vintage cars and trucks on the walls and regulars who've been coming since the beginning.

It's the kind of place where the coffee's already poured before you sit down, and where a single-location diner has earned the loyalty most chains only wish for. This concept is built around that story — not a generic template.
