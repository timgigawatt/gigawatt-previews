Why Moon

A husband-and-wife practice that cares for the whole family

Dr. Joe Moon opened Moon Orthodontics in 2016 to do orthodontics the personal way — an independent, locally owned practice where you see the same doctor at every visit, not a rotating roster.

He trained as both a general dentist and an orthodontist (University of Pennsylvania and University of Minnesota), and is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists. Right alongside him, his wife Dr. Beverly Moon runs the family's general dentistry practice — so cleanings, checkups, and straightening live under one trusted roof.

It's the rare setup where your dentist and your orthodontist actually talk to each other. That's better coordination, fewer surprises, and one team that already knows your smile.
