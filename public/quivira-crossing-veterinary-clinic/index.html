A legacy in good hands

New owners. The same clinic you've always trusted.

When Drs. Nathan & Marci Feyerabend took the reins at Quivira Crossing, longtime clients worried the little things might change. They didn't. The transition has been so smooth that most families only noticed the care got more personal.

Both doctors trained at the University of Missouri College of Veterinary Medicine and have volunteered their skills internationally. Their promise on 135th Street is simple: treat every patient as though it were their own.
