The story behind the truck

Built from a truck, a layoff, and a lot of grit

In 2019, Andrey Portnoy lost his job. With a truck, a little savings, and zero garage-door experience, he bet on himself instead of waiting for the next opening — learning the trade through YouTube, research, and long days on real jobs.

"With grit and resourcefulness, you can build something from the ground up."

That solo operation grew into a trusted KC-metro team. The values never changed: be transparent, fix the whole system instead of chasing quick patches, and teach homeowners how their door actually works. It's why neighbors keep calling back — and sending their friends.
